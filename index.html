Hooksy

How to play

  • Every day there are 3 secret words. Everyone gets the same ones.
  • Press Begin Reeling to start a round. You get 60 seconds and 5 guesses.
  • You start with one hint. Each wrong guess reveals another, up to 5 hints.
  • Reloading the page during a round loses that round, so stay on the line!

Scoring (up to 9 per round)

Correct word+3
Speedunder 10s +3 · under 20s +2 · under 30s +1
Guesses1st +3 · 2nd +2 · 3rd +1

If you miss the word, the round scores 0.
